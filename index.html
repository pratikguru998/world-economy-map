Where the world's ~ a year comes from: economies → industries → companies

Click a slice to zoom in · click the centre to go back · hover for details

    How to read it. Ring 1 is the economy (nominal GDP), ring 2 its industries (share of value added), ring 3 the biggest companies in that industry, sized by annual revenue. For the five largest economies (US, China, Germany, Japan, India) industries split further into sub-industries before the companies. Revenue is gross sales, not value added, so big firms can out-size their industry's GDP share — those slices are scaled down to fit and marked with ⚠. “Rest of …” slices are the millions of smaller firms, public bodies and households.

    Sources (approximate, rounded). GDP: IMF World Economic Outlook, 2024. Industry shares: World Bank, OECD and national accounts. Company revenue: company reports and the Fortune Global 500, ~FY2024. Figures are for orientation, not investment decisions.
