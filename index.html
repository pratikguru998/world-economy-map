Where the world's ~ a year comes from: economies → industries → companies

Click a slice to zoom in · click the centre to go back · hover for details

    How to read it. Ring 1 is the economy (nominal GDP), ring 2 its industries (share of value added), ring 3 the biggest companies in that industry, sized by annual revenue. Industries split into sub-industries before the companies. For the five largest economies (US, China, Germany, Japan, India) sub-industry sizes come from national accounts; elsewhere they're estimated from company revenues. Revenue is gross sales, not value added, so big firms can out-size their industry's GDP share — those slices are scaled down to fit and marked with ⚠. “Rest of …” slices are the millions of smaller firms, public bodies and households.

    Sources (approximate, rounded). GDP: IMF World Economic Outlook, 2024. Industry shares: World Bank, OECD and national accounts; India's farm output by crop and product: MoSPI value of output from agriculture and allied sectors, 2023-24 (finer splits estimated). Companies: about 3,200 firms from screener.in (India's ~300 largest listed companies by trailing-12-month sales), hand-curated company reports (~FY2024), Wikipedia's "largest companies" lists (CC BY-SA 4.0) and Wikidata (CC0), converted to US dollars at approximate 2023-24 rates. Each company's panel shows its source and year. Figures are for orientation, not investment decisions.
